# Credits

Mergefall uses a curated subset of purchased pixel-art assets by ElvGames and ElvGames & Pegonthetrack.

## Artwork

- Swordtember 2024 by ElvGames: sword rank icons used for tile progression.
- Free Inventory from User Interface by ElvGames: inventory-style panel, slot, selection, and bar textures used for the fantasy armory interface.
- Pixel VFX by ElvGames: small merge, impact, and game-over feedback sprites.
- Neatpixels fonts by ElvGames: fantasy display, UI, and numeric fonts.

## Audio

- Universal Sound Effects by ElvGames & Pegonthetrack: short movement, drop, landing, merge, new-run, blocked, and game-over cues.

The asset packs are not original Mergefall artwork. Applicable license copies for the curated imported assets are stored in `assets/licenses/`.
